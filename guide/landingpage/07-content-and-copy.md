# 7. Content and Copy Guide

## 7.1 Voice

- Professional, precise, welcoming and academically literate.
- Explain benefits in plain language.
- Avoid hype such as “revolutionary”, “perfect”, “zero errors”, “instant publishing” or “the world's best”.
- Avoid guaranteeing publication acceptance or research quality.

## 7.2 Proposed home page copy

### Hero

Eyebrow: `A focused workspace for academic writing`

Headline: `Research writing, from source to finished paper.`

Supporting copy:
`Write in LaTeX, organize your research files, compile with a managed workflow, and review your PDF in one focused workspace. Bring your co-authors and research tools into the process as your work grows.`

Primary CTA: `Get started`
Secondary CTA: `Explore the demo`

Microcopy: `A clearer workflow for papers, theses and technical documents.`

### Feature section

Heading: `Everything around the writing workflow`

Intro:
`The work behind a research paper involves more than typing. Keep source files, references, figures, compilation and review connected in one place.`

Feature descriptions:
- **LaTeX workspace:** `Organize project files and edit structured documents in a focused source editor.`
- **PDF preview:** `Review the typeset document alongside your source and inspect build diagnostics.`
- **Bibliography workflow:** `Keep citations and bibliography files close to the manuscript they support.`
- **Co-author review:** `Coordinate feedback, comments and revisions with collaborators as collaboration features become available.`
- **AI assistance:** `Use AI suggestions to understand issues and propose edits. Review every change before applying it.`
- **Visual academic tools:** `Create structured tables, diagrams and other research visuals through guided tools.`
- **Import and export:** `Bring existing work into the workflow and export editable project files.`
- **Equation capture:** `Turn suitable equation images into editable LaTeX candidates with human verification.`

Only publish descriptions for capabilities that are actually available or clearly label them as beta/planned.

### Workflow

Heading: `From first draft to a review-ready PDF`

Steps:
1. `Start with a project` — Choose a template or create a clean workspace.
2. `Write and organize` — Keep source, figures and bibliography together.
3. `Compile and inspect` — Build the document and review the resulting PDF and diagnostics.
4. `Review and refine` — Make revisions, collect feedback and export your work.

### AI section

Heading: `AI that assists. You stay in control.`

Copy:
`Ask for help understanding a compilation error, reviewing a section or proposing a focused revision. Suggestions should be presented clearly so you can inspect, accept or reject each change.`

### Final CTA

Heading: `Give your research a more focused writing space.`
Body: `Start a project, explore the workflow, or talk with us about your research team's needs.`

## 7.3 Feature status labels

- Available
- Beta
- Planned

Use the label in text, not only colour. Admin-configurable feature status must match release truth.

## 7.4 Testimonials and metrics

Do not create synthetic testimonials attributed to real people. Do not invent:
- number of users
- number of papers compiled
- uptime percentages
- customer logos
- institutional deployments
- review scores
- security certifications

Use product screenshots and clearly labelled sample content instead.
